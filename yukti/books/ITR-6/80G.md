# The book of Schedule 80G — Donations to funds and charitable institutions · ITR-6, A.Y. 2026-27

Read row by row from the utility's **80G** sheet (71 rows, no hidden rows) and
confirmed against the CBDT ITR-6 schema's `Schedule80G`. Every bucket, label,
dropdown and formula below is the department's own.

---

## 1 · Purpose — donations in four rate/limit buckets

Schedule 80G holds the **details of donations entitled for deduction under
section 80G**, donee by donee, sorted into **four buckets** by the two axes that
decide how much is deductible — the rate (100% or 50%) and whether a **qualifying
limit** applies. The consolidated eligible amount feeds **Schedule VI-A Part B,
item a (80G)** at Sl. No. 1a. The sheet's title: *"Details of donations entitled
for deduction under section 80G."*

The four buckets, with the utility's own bucket letter, the label, and the
schema object each maps to:

| Bucket | Sheet label | Schema object | Sheet named range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Donations entitled for 100% deduction **without** qualifying limit | `Don100Percent` | `Per10080G` |
| **B** | Donations entitled for 50% deduction **without** qualifying limit | `Don50PercentNoApprReqd` | `PerNO5080G` |
| **C** | Donations entitled for 100% deduction **subject to** qualifying limit | `Don100PercentApprReqd` | `PerYES10080G` |
| **D** | Donations entitled for 50% deduction **subject to** qualifying limit | `Don50PercentApprReqd` | `Per5080G` |
| **E** | **Total Amount of Donations (Aix + Bix + Cix + Dix)** | the four `TotalDonationsUs80G*` totals | — |

Each bucket's full sheet heading adds: *"(where any row is filled by the user,
all the fields in that row should become mandatory)"* — i.e. filling one cell of
a donee row makes the whole row required.

## 3 · The eligible-amount formulas — the ₹2,000 cash cap and the qualifying limit

The ₹2,000 cash cap runs through every bucket: any single cash donation over
₹2,000 is disallowed (`IF(L>2000,0,L)`). Rule **A809** — *amount donated in cash
should not exceed Rs. 2000.*

Per-bucket eligible-amount cell (row 7/21/35/49) and totals:

| Bucket | Per-row eligible (Q) | Total eligible |
|---|---|---|
| **A** (100%, no limit) | `MIN(SUM(IF(L7>2000,0,L7),M7),TotalPendingIncome)` — full amount | Q13 = `MIN(SUM(Per10080G.EligibleAmt),TotalPendingIncome)` |
| **B** (50%, no limit) | `ROUND(MIN(SUM(IF(L21>2000,0,L21),M21))/2,TotalPendingIncome),0)` — half | Q27 |
| **C** (100%, with limit) | `ROUND(MIN(MIN(eligible,TotalPendingIncome),QualifyingAmount80G),0)` — full, capped at the qualifying limit | Q41 = `ROUND(MIN(C_Eligible,CD_EligibleAmount),0)` |
| **D** (50%, with limit) | `ROUND(MIN(TotalPendingIncome,MIN(CDE_EligibleAmount,eligible/2)),0)` — half, capped at the residual limit | Q55 |

Row totals per bucket (L/M/P = cash / other mode / total; the four
`TotDon…` and `TotElg…` schema totals):

| Bucket | Cash total | Other-mode total | Donation total | Eligible total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** (r13) | `TotDon100PercentCash` | `TotDon100PercentOtherMode` | `TotDon100Percent` | `TotElgDon100Percent` |
| **B** (r27) | `TotDon50PercentNoApprReqdCash` | `TotDon50PercentNoApprReqdOtherMode` | `TotDon50PercentNoApprReqd` | `TotElgDon50PercentNoApprReqd` |
| **C** (r41) | `TotDon100PercentApprReqdCash` | `TotDon100PercentApprReqdOtherMode` | `TotDon100Percent` | `TotElgDon100Percent` |
| **D** (r55) | `TotDon50PercentApprReqdCash` | `TotDon50PercentApprReqdOtherMode` | `TotDon50PercentApprReqd` | `TotElgDon50PercentApprReqd` |

**E — grand totals (row 60):** `TotalDonationsUs80GCash`, `TotalDonationsUs80GOtherMode`,
`TotalDonationsUs80G` (= Total Amount of Donations, Aix + Bix + Cix + Dix), and
`TotalEligibleDonationsUs80G`. Rule **A811** — E total = Aix + Bix + Cix + Dx.

## 4 · The qualifying-limit working (helper columns T–X, rows 3–9)

Buckets C and D are capped at the **qualifying limit = 10% of adjusted total
income**. The utility computes it in a side block:

| Cell | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V3 | Qualifying Amount 80G C | `ROUND(0.1*MAX(0,(TotalPendingIncome − (certain Part-C deductions))),0)` — **10% of** adjusted total income |
| V5 / V7 | 10% of · **Total Income −** | the base of the 10% qualifying amount |
| V8 | **Income from 80 IA (Sch VIA 80GGA/B/C) +** | add-back into the base |
| V9 | **Income from 80 IA (Sch VIA PART C(c-J)** | Part-C deductions removed from the base |
| T3 / T4 | Sum of Eligible Amount 80G C | `SUM(PerYES10080G.EligibleAmt)` — bucket C's raw eligible |
| U3 / U4 | Sum of Eligible Amount 80G D | `SUM(Per5080G.EligibleAmt)` — bucket D's raw eligible |
| W3 / W4 | Eligible Amount 80G D | `QualifyingAmount80G` residual after C |
| X3 / X4 | Eligible Amount 80G E | `MAX(0,ROUND((CD_EligibleAmount − C_Eligible)/2,0))` |
| X7 / X8 | Eligible Amount 80G D − · **Sum of Amount 80G C** | the residual-limit arithmetic for D |

The mechanism: 100%-with-limit (C) draws against the 10% qualifying amount first;
50%-with-limit (D) draws against whatever qualifying amount is left, at half rate.
Rule **A812** fires when the assessee claims 80G *more than the qualifying limit.*

## 6 · Cross-sheet feeds

| Direction | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **In** | `TotalPendingIncome`, `QualifyingAmount80G`, the Part-C deduction figures | Part B-TI / Schedule VI-A |
| **Out** | `TotalEligibleDonationsUs80G` (E) | Schedule VI-A Part B item **a (80G)**, Sl. No. 1a (`I48`/`K48`) |

Rule **A814/A815** — the system-calculated 80G value at VI-A Sl. No. 1a must
equal the eligible-donation total at E in Schedule 80G. Rule **A806** — if 80G is
claimed at VI-A Sl. No. (a), Schedule 80G is mandatory. Rule **A815** — the 80G
deduction under Part B **cannot be claimed if the new tax regime (115BAA or
115BAB) is selected.**

## 7 · What is mandatory (schema `required`)

On `Schedule80G` itself: **`TotalDonationsUs80GCash`, `TotalDonationsUs80GOtherMode`,
`TotalDonationsUs80G`, `TotalEligibleDonationsUs80G`** (the four E totals).

On each bucket object: its four totals — for A/C `TotDon100PercentCash`,
`TotDon100PercentOtherMode`, `TotDon100Percent`, `TotElgDon100Percent` (and the
approval-required variants `TotDon100PercentApprReqdCash`,
`TotDon100PercentApprReqdOtherMode`); for B/D
`TotDon50PercentNoApprReqdCash`, `TotDon50PercentNoApprReqdOtherMode`,
`TotDon50PercentNoApprReqd`, `TotElgDon50PercentNoApprReqd`, and
`TotDon50PercentApprReqdCash`, `TotDon50PercentApprReqdOtherMode`,
`TotDon50PercentApprReqd`, `TotElgDon50PercentApprReqd`.

On each `DoneeDetail` row: `DoneeName`, `DoneePAN`, `AddressDetail.AddrDetail`,
`AddressDetail.CityOrTownOrDistrict`, `AddressDetail.StateCode`,
`AddressDetail.PinCode`, `DonationAmtCash`, `DonationAmtOtherMode`, `DonationAmt`,
`DonationElgAmt`. Optional per row: `TransactionRefNum`, `IFSCCode`, `ArnNbr`
(written when carrying a value; `TransactionRefNum`/`IFSCCode` become mandatory
for other-mode donations, rule A816).

| Where | Repeatable? |
|---|---|
| Donee rows in each bucket A/B/C/D (`DoneeDetail`) | **yes** — 5 shipped per bucket, addable |
| Per-bucket totals (rows 13/27/41/55) | one figure each — computed |
| Grand totals E (row 60) | one figure each — computed |
| The qualifying-limit working (T–X) | computed side block, not filed |

---

## 9 · What ITR-6 has here that ITR-2 does not

The 80G schedule itself is common to both forms, but on ITR-6 the qualifying
limit's base is the **company's** adjusted total income (10% of total income
after removing the profit-linked Part-C deductions — 80-IA and its family), and
the whole Part-B 80G deduction is **switched off under the concessional company
regimes 115BAA / 115BAB** (rule A815). ITR-2 has no such regime interaction. The
PAN-uniqueness rule A813 — the same PAN cannot appear in two of the four buckets —
also applies.

---

## 10 · What this means for the build

1. **Four repeatable buckets A/B/C/D**, each the same donee grid, 5 rows shipped;
   only bucket D carries the ARN column.
2. **Cash over ₹2,000 drops out; totals and eligible amounts are computed** and
   green. C and D are capped at the 10% qualifying limit, C drawing first.
3. **State is `StateWithoutForeign`** (37 values) — the RA/80G list, not the
   80GGA list.
4. **E feeds VI-A Sl. No. 1a** and must equal it (A814); block the whole Part-B
   80G claim under 115BAA/115BAB (A815).
5. **Enforce PAN uniqueness across buckets** (A813) and other-mode ⇒
   TransactionRefNum + IFSC mandatory (A816).
