# The book of Schedule 80LA — ITR-6, A.Y. 2026-27

Read row by row from the utility's **80LA** sheet, and confirmed against the CBDT
ITR-6 schema (`Schedule80LA`) and the validation-rules document. Section 80LA is
the **deduction in respect of an Offshore Banking Unit or a Unit of an
International Financial Services Centre (IFSC)** — a scheduled/foreign bank with
an OBU in an SEZ (80LA(1)) or an IFSC unit (80LA(1A)).

---

## 1 · The shape of the schedule

One repeatable table, headed **Schedule 80LA — Deduction in respect of offshore
banking unit or IFSC**. The utility gives entry rows 8–11, a Total row (13), and
a hidden helper row (5). Column heads are on row 7 (each carrying its own item
number 1–9).

| Item (row 7) | Column head | Cell |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sl. No. | B |
| 2 | Sub-section in which deduction is claimed | C |
| 3 | Type of Entity | D |
| 4 | Type of income of the unit | E |
| 5 | Authority granting registration | F |
| 6 | Date of registration | G |
| 7 | Registration number | H |
| 8 | First AY during which deduction is claimed | I |
| 9 | Amount of deduction claimed for current AY | J |

Row 4 carries **Schedule 80LA** and *"Deduction in respect of offshore banking
unit or IFSC"*.

---

## 2 · The items, row by row

Item numbers are the sheet's own 1–9 (confirmed against the rules document, which
refers to "Sl. No. 1 to 7" and "Sl. No. 8"):

| Item | Label | Type / enum | Schema key | Hidden? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sl. No. | auto int (`B9 = B8+1`) | (index of `Schedule80LADtls[]`) | no |
| 2 | Sub-section in which deduction is claimed | enum, see below | `SubSecDedClmd` | no |
| 3 | Type of Entity | enum, see below | `EntityType` | no |
| 4 | Type of income of the unit | enum, see below | `IncmTypeUnt` | no |
| 5 | Authority granting registration | enum (SEBI/IFSCA/RBI) | `RegGNTAuth` | no |
| 6 | Date of registration | date `DD/MM/YYYY` (schema: on/after 2024-04-01) | `RegDate` | no |
| 7 | Registration number | text (max 30) | `RegNumber` | no |
| 8 | First AY during which deduction is claimed | text (max 7) | `FstAYDeduction` | no |
| 9 | Amount of deduction claimed for current AY | amount ≥ 0 | `AmtDedCurAY` | no |
| — | Total | computed | `Total` | no |

Total row 13: `J13 = SUM(AmountDedClamied_80LA)` (schema key `Total`).

Two off-table helper cells on row 7–9 carry the per-sub-section subtotals used by
Schedule VI-A: **TotalAmountDed_80LA1** (`V`) = `SUMIF(Subsection_80LA, "1. 80LA(1)", AmountDedClamied_80LA)`
and **TotalAmountDed_80LA1A** (`W`) = `SUMIF(Subsection_80LA, "2. 80LA(1A)", AmountDedClamied_80LA)`.
These are named subtotals, not filed rows of their own.

### Hidden row

- **Row 5 (hidden)** — "Sub-section in which deduction is claimed" with the value
  "1. 80LA(1)" (cells C5 / J5): the old single-value control, superseded by the
  per-row dropdown on column C. Listed as **hidden — not built**.

## 3 · The dropdowns and their enums

Data-validation lists actually present on the sheet:

| Column | Cells | Values on the sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-section (C, and hidden J5) | C8:C11 | `(Select)`, `1. 80LA(1)`, `2. 80LA(1A)` |
| Authority granting registration (F) | F8:F11 | `(Select)`, `1.SEBI`, `2.IFSCA`, `3.RBI` |
| Type of Entity (D) | D8:D11 | `(Select)` (dependent list — full values from schema enum below) |
| Type of income of the unit (E) | E8:E11 | `(Select)` (dependent list — full values from schema enum below) |

`Type of Entity` and `Type of income of the unit` are **cascading dropdowns**
enabled by the chosen sub-section (rules A606/A607); the sheet's data-validation
resolves only the `(Select)` placeholder, the real options come from the schema:

**`SubSecDedClmd`** enum[2]: `80LA(1)`, `80LA(1A)`.

**`EntityType`** enum[3]:
| code | meaning |
|---|---|
| SchdBankSEZ | Scheduled bank having Overseas Banking Unit in SEZ |
| FrgnBankSEZ | Any foreign bank having Overseas Banking Unit in SEZ |
| UntIFSC | A Unit of IFSC |

**`IncmTypeUnt`** enum[4]:
| code | meaning |
|---|---|
| OffshoreBnkng | From an Offshore Banking Unit in a Special Economic Zone |
| Sec10Of1949 | From the business under s.6(1) of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 |
| IFSCSplEcoZone | From any Unit of the IFSC approved for setting up in that centre |
| TnfrAsst1949 | Arising from the transfer of an asset (aircraft or ship) leased by a unit |

**`RegGNTAuth`** enum[3]: `SEBI`, `IFSCA`, `RBI`.

## 4 · Cross-sheet feeds

**In**: Part A General — 80LA(1A) is allowed only if *"Whether assessee is
located in an International Financial Services Centre and derives income solely in
convertible foreign exchange?"* is **Yes** (rules A839/A840).

**Out**: the two per-sub-section subtotals feed **Schedule VI-A** at
**Sl. No. 2d** — `TotalAmountDed_80LA1` → the 80-LA(1) line, `TotalAmountDed_80LA1A`
→ the 80-LA(1A) line.

---

## 5 · The rules the sheet enforces

- **A604** — if amount claimed > 0, the sub-section (item 2) must be selected.
- **A605** — if the amount at Sl. No. 8/9 > 0, items 1 to 7 must all be filled.
- **A606** — Type of Entity is enabled based on the sub-section chosen.
- **A607** — Type of income of the unit is enabled based on the sub-section chosen.
- **A608** — the 80-LA(1) value in Schedule VI-A (Sl. No. 2d) cannot exceed the
  80LA(1) amount here (Sl. No. 8).
- **A609** — 80LA(1) claimed in VI-A but Schedule 80LA not filled → error.
- **A610** — the 80-LA(1A) value in VI-A cannot exceed the 80LA(1A) amount here.
- **A611** — 80LA(1A) claimed in VI-A but Schedule 80LA not filled → error.
- **A838** — in Schedule VI-A, both 80LA(1) and 80LA(1A) cannot be claimed
  together.
- **A839/A840** — 80LA(1A) / 80LA(1) allowed only on the IFSC / convertible-
  foreign-exchange conditions selected in Part A General.

---

## 6 · What this means for the build

1. A repeatable table of items 1–9 → `Schedule80LADtls[]`, with `Total` =
   sum of amounts.
2. Dropdowns: sub-section (2 values), authority (SEBI/IFSCA/RBI); entity and
   income-type as **cascading** dropdowns whose options depend on the chosen
   sub-section (A606/A607), seeded from the schema enums.
3. Compute the two subtotals `TotalAmountDed_80LA1` / `TotalAmountDed_80LA1A` and
   feed them to VI-A Sl. No. 2d; enforce the "not both together" (A838) and the
   not-more-than caps (A608/A610).
4. Do not build the hidden row 5.
