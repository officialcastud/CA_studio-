# The book of Schedule 80M — ITR-6, A.Y. 2026-27

Read row by row from the utility's **80M** sheet, and confirmed against the CBDT
ITR-6 schema (`ScheduleVIA` → `UsrDeductUndChapVIA.Section80MDtls[]`) and the
validation-rules document. Section 80M is the company-only deduction for
**inter-corporate dividends**: a domestic company that receives a dividend and
itself distributes a dividend may deduct the received dividend, but only up to
what it distributes in time — this sheet is where that distribution is proved.

---

## 1 · The shape of the schedule

The 80M sheet is a **single small table** (utility rows 1–5, three data rows
3–5, rows can be added in the live form). It does **not** compute the deduction
itself; it records the *dividend the company distributed* and *the schedule under
which the corresponding dividend income is offered*. The deduction figure and
its OS/BP split are computed on the **VIA** sheet (rows 65–67) and land in
`ScheduleVIA`. So this book covers the 80M input table plus the 80M lines of
Schedule VI-A that consume it.

| Utility cell | Sheet row 2 header (column) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A2 | **Sl. No.** | serial, auto |
| B2 | **Date of distribution of Dividend** | date the company paid its own dividend |
| C2 | **Amount of dividend distributed** | amount distributed on that date |
| D2 | **Schedule under which Dividend income is offered** | dropdown — where the *received* dividend income sits (ScheduleOS or ScheduleBP) |

Row 1 carries the titles **Schedule 80M** and **Table for Schedule 80M**.

---

## 2 · The items, row by row (the entry table)

Each live row (3, 4, 5 …) is one distribution event:

| Item | Label | Type / enum | Schema key | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | Sl. No. | auto integer | (index of `Section80MDtls[]`) | column A |
| — | Date of distribution of Dividend | date `DD/MM/YYYY` | `Section80MDate` | column B (`Sch80M.DateDistDiv` = `'80M'!$B$3:$B$5`) |
| — | Amount of dividend distributed | amount ≥ 0 | `Section80MAmnt` | column C (`Sch80M.DistDivAmt` = `'80M'!$C$3:$C$5`) |
| — | Schedule under which Dividend income is offered | enum, see below | `Section80MType` | column D (`Sch80M.DivIncOffered` = `'80M'!$D$3:$D$5`) |

These three columns are `Section80MDtls[]` — an unbounded array, one object per
distribution row.

### Dropdown — column D (`Section80MType`)

Data-validation list on cells **D3:D5**: `(Select), ScheduleOS, ScheduleBP`.
The schema enum on `Section80MType` is exactly two values:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ScheduleOS** | the received dividend income is offered under Schedule OS |
| **ScheduleBP** | the received dividend income is offered under Schedule BP |

`(Select)` is the empty placeholder. Rule A844 makes the choice mandatory
whenever 80M is claimed.

## 3 · The Schedule VI-A lines that consume this table

The deduction itself is item **o** of **Part C** of Schedule VI-A (VIA sheet
D65 = "o", *"80M — Deduction in respect of certain inter-corporate dividends"*),
split into two sub-lines A (ScheduleOS) and B (ScheduleBP):

| Schema key | Named range | Derivation (VIA sheet) |
|---|---|---|
| `Section80M` | `scvia.Section80M` = VIA!I65 | total 80M deduction claimed = `Section80M_OS + Section80M_BP` |
| `Section80M_OS` | `scvia.Section80MOS` = VIA!I66 | 80M attributable to dividend income offered under OS |
| `Section80M_BP` | `scvia.Section80MBP` = VIA!I67 | 80M attributable to dividend income offered under BP; `= SUMIF(Sch80M.DivIncOffered,"ScheduleBP", DistDivAmt)` capped |

The **system-calculated** column K mirrors these (K65 `Section80M_Calc`, K66
`Section80MOS_Calc`, K67 `Section80MBP_Calc`, P67 `Section80MNew`), and the
user-enterable I column is capped by it — the "user enterable amount" rule A864.

### Part C totals (required schema keys the gate checks)

These three keys of `ScheduleVIA` (both under `UsrDeductUndChapVIA` and the
system `DeductUndChapVIA`) are **required** and carry the 80M figure into the
chapter VI-A totals:

| Schema key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `TotPartBchapterVIA` | total of Part B deductions (payments) |
| `TotPartCchapterVIA` | total of Part C deductions (incomes) — **includes item o, 80M** (rule A850: Part C = e + … + o + …) |
| `TotalChapVIADeductions` | total chapter VI-A deduction = Part B + Part C |

## 4 · The computation method and the cap (read carefully)

80M is a **capped** deduction. From the VIA formulas (rows 65–67) and the rules:

1. **Base amount** — the deduction is the dividend the company *distributed*,
   bucketed by the schedule the *received* income is offered under
   (`SUMIF(Sch80M.DivIncOffered,"ScheduleBP",…)` and the OS equivalent).
2. **Cap 1 — cannot exceed the dividend income offered.** Rule A841/A842:
   *"Deduction claimed u/s 80M cannot exceed dividend income offered in schedule
   OS and schedule BP subject to maximum of balance income at Sl. no. xiii(5) and
   ii(5) of schedule BFLA."* The BP line K67 takes `MIN(PL.Dividends +
   PartAPL.DividendIncome…, distributed amount)`; the OS line is the analogue.
3. **Cap 2 — time limit on distribution.** Rule A837: the *date of distribution
   of dividend* (column B) cannot be after **one month prior to the due date for
   furnishing the return under section 139(1)**. A distribution later than that
   does not qualify.
4. **Belated return bar.** The formulas gate on `IF(MID(ReturnFileSec,1,6)=
   "139(4)",0,…)` — a **belated return under 139(4)** gets **0** for 80M.
5. **Foreign company bar.** Rule A842: *"Foreign company cannot claim deduction
   u/s 80M."* When `DomesticCompFlg = "No"` the formula yields 0.
6. **Mandatory dropdown.** Rule A844: if 80M is claimed, one of `ScheduleOS` /
   `ScheduleBP` must be selected on column D.
7. **Concessional-regime exception.** Under 115BAA / 115BAB most chapter VI-A
   deductions are barred, but rules A849/A850 list **80M as an allowed
   exception** — 80M survives the concessional regimes (unlike Schedule 80,
   10AA, etc.).

`VIA80MAL` (VIA!P61) and `VIA80MBL` (VIA!Q61) are the helper flags the VIA sheet
uses to switch the OS/BP allowability on and off inside these formulas.

---

## 5 · Cross-sheet feeds

**In** (what fills this table / its caps):
- The received dividend income figures — **Schedule OS** (dividend rows) and
  **Schedule BP / Part A-P&L** `Dividends` — set the cap.
- **Part A General** — `DomesticCompFlg` (domestic vs foreign) and
  `ReturnFileSec` (139(1) vs 139(4)) gate allowability.
- **Schedule BFLA** balance income at xiii(5) and ii(5) sets the outer cap.

**Out** (what this table feeds):
- `ScheduleVIA.UsrDeductUndChapVIA.Section80MDtls[]` (the distribution rows) and
  `Section80M`, `Section80M_OS`, `Section80M_BP` (VI-A Part C item o).
- Through `TotPartCchapterVIA` → `TotalChapVIADeductions` → **Part B-TI** total
  income.

---

## 6 · Hidden rows and unreadable sources

- No hidden rows on the 80M sheet within the checked column range.
- The 80M data rows carry **no on-sheet formula** — the entry cells (B/C/D 3:5)
  are plain inputs; all computation is on the VIA sheet, read above. Nothing was
  guessed; every cap is taken from a named formula or a numbered rule.

---

## 7 · What this means for the build

1. Build a **repeatable three-column table** — Date of distribution, Amount of
   dividend distributed, Schedule under which dividend income is offered
   (dropdown ScheduleOS / ScheduleBP) — exporting to `Section80MDtls[]`.
2. Compute `Section80M_OS` and `Section80M_BP` by SUMIF on the schedule chosen,
   each **capped at the dividend income offered** under that head, then
   `Section80M = Section80M_OS + Section80M_BP`.
3. Enforce the caps as live checks: foreign company → 0 (A842); 139(4) → 0;
   distribution date ≤ one month before the 139(1) due date (A837); dropdown
   mandatory when claimed (A844); claimed ≤ user-enterable/system amount (A864).
4. Keep 80M **allowed under 115BAA/115BAB** (the one Part-C survivor), unlike the
   other chapter VI-A deductions.
5. Feed the result into VI-A Part C item **o**, and thence into
   `TotPartCchapterVIA` / `TotalChapVIADeductions`.
