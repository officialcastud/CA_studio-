# The book of Schedule 112A — ITR-6, A.Y. 2026-27

Read row by row from the utility's **112A** sheet (a.k.a. "Tool-112A", rows 3–15;
4 live rows, 3 hidden) and confirmed against the CBDT ITR-6 schema block
**Schedule112A**. This is the **scrip-by-scrip** working for long-term capital
gain on equity shares / units of an equity-oriented fund / units of a business
trust **on which STT has been paid**, taxed under section 112A. Its column-14
totals feed **Schedule CG head B4**.

Nothing here is invented; every heading and column is the department's own.
Appendix 3 reproduces every live row verbatim; Appendix 2 lists every schema
leaf.

---

## 1 · Purpose and shape

One flat table, one row per scrip/lot, unlimited rows (addable). Row 3 is the
title, "From sale of equity share in a company or unit of equity oriented fund or
unit of a business trust on which STT is paid u/s 112A". Row 4 is the column
header (18 columns, numbered Col 1 … Col 14 in row 5). Row 12 is "Total of each
column". Rows 13–15 are **hidden** aggregate lines (before / on-or-after 23rd
July 2024 splits and the total LTCG u/s 112A).

The whole detail array is `Schedule112ADtls[]`; the column totals are the
`*112A` scalar keys.

**Section 55(2)(ac) grandfathering:** column 7 (cost without indexation) is the
higher of the actual cost (col 8) and, for assets bought on or before 31.01.2018,
the lower of the 31-Jan-2018 FMV (col 11) and the sale consideration (col 6) —
column 9. That is the grandfathering of gains up to 31 January 2018.

### Column totals (row 12, "Total of each column") — scalar keys

`SaleValue112A` (Σ col 6) · `CostAcqWithoutIndx112A` (Σ col 7) ·
`AcquisitionCost112A` (Σ col 8) · `LTCGBeforelowerB1B2112A` (Σ col 9) ·
`FairMktValueCapAst112A` (Σ col 11) · `ExpExclCnctTransfer112A` (Σ) ·
`Deductions112A` (Σ col 13) · `Balance112A` (Σ col 14).

Hidden aggregate rows (not built): 13 "(i) Total of column (14) where transfer
was before 23rd July 2024"; 14 "(ii) Total of column (14) where transfer was on
or after 23rd July 2024"; 15 "(iii) Total of LTCG u/s 112A".

---

## 3 · Dropdown

Column 1a (E6:E10) — **WHETHER SHARE ACQUIRED**: `(i) On or Before 31st January
2018` · `(ii) After 31st January 2018`.

Column F (Share/Unit transferred before / on or after 23rd July 2024) is a
before/after flag (no enum resolved) that drives the hidden 23-July split totals.

---

## 4 · Cross-sheet feeds

**Out:** `Balance112A` (Σ column 14) → **Schedule CG head B4** ("Long-term
Capital Gains on sale of capital assets at B4 above (column 14 of Schedule
112A)"); the before / on-or-after 23-July split (hidden rows 13–14) → B4a i / ii.
The intermediate columns 12/13 feed the LTCG cost/consideration lines of Schedule
CG referenced in the headers as "item 5(b)(i)(B)(2)" and "item 5(b)(ii)".

**In:** per-scrip data is entered directly (or imported); no other schedule feeds
112A.
