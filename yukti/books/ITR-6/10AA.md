# The book of Schedule 10AA — Deduction for SEZ units · ITR-6, A.Y. 2026-27

Read row by row from the utility's **10AA** sheet (15 rows, of which rows 1–7 are
hidden) and confirmed against the CBDT ITR-6 schema's `Schedule10AA`. Every
label, dropdown and formula below is the department's own.

---

## 1 · Purpose — the export-profit deduction for Special Economic Zone units

Schedule 10AA computes the **deduction under section 10AA in respect of units
located in a Special Economic Zone**. It is *not* a Chapter VI-A deduction — it
is taken against total income after gross total income and stands alongside the
Chapter VI-A block. The sheet is a small register: one line per SEZ undertaking,
each with the assessment year the unit began to manufacture/produce/provide
service and the amount of deduction, plus a computed total.

This schedule is why Part B-TI states its total income as *"GTI minus Chapter
VI-A deductions **& deduction u/s 10AA**"* (validation rule A736): the 10AA
deduction is subtracted separately from Chapter VI-A.

deduction). Logged as excluded: *hidden superseded Schedule 10A*.

## 6 · What is mandatory (schema)

`Schedule10AA` itself has **no top-level `required` list**. Within it, once an
undertaking row exists the schema requires `AssmtYrUnit` and `DedUs10Sub` on the
`DedFromUndertakingWithAy` row, and `TotalDedUs10Sub` on the `DedUs10Detail`
object. The nesting is deep: `DeductSEZ → DedUs10Detail → Undertaking →
DedFromUndertakingWithAy[]`.

---

## 7 · What repeats and what does not

| Where | Repeatable? |
|---|---|
| Undertaking rows (`DedFromUndertakingWithAy`) | **yes** — one per SEZ unit, addable |
| Total (F15 / J15) | one figure — computed |

---

## 8 · What ITR-6 has here that ITR-2 does not

ITR-2 has **no Schedule 10AA** — the SEZ export-profit deduction is a
business/company relief. In the ITR-2 build the stray "10AA card" was deleted for
exactly this reason. On ITR-6 it is a **legitimate, live schedule**, built here,
subtracted at Part B-TI separately from Chapter VI-A.

---

## 9 · What this means for the build

1. **Build only the visible Schedule 10AA (rows 11–15)**; do not build the hidden
   Schedule 10A (rows 1–7).
2. **One repeatable undertaking row**, each with the AY dropdown and an amount;
   the total is computed and green.
3. **The AY dropdown is an inline 16-year list** ending 2022-23 — build it from
   the sheet's own inline list, matching the `AssmtYrUnit` enum.
4. **Subtract at Part B-TI** as a separate line from Chapter VI-A (rule A736).
