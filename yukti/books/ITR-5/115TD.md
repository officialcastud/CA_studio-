# Schedule 115TD — Accreted income & tax thereon u/s 115TD (trusts/institutions)

Block: `Schedule115TD`. Section: `other`. Sheet tab: `115TD`.
Charges **additional income-tax on the accreted income** of a specified person (trust/institution registered u/s 12AA/12AB/10(23C) etc.) on conversion, merger or non-transfer of assets on dissolution. Accreted income = aggregate FMV of total assets − total liability (with carve-outs), taxed at the **maximum marginal rate** u/s 115TD, plus **interest u/s 115TE** at 1% per month, with a challan-wise table of tax deposited.

## The shape

- A header at `[D5]/[F5]` — *"Schedule115TD / Accreted income under section 115TD"*.
- **A single computation column** running `r6:r20` — one figure per line (fields 1–12). The user-visible label sits in column **F**, and each value/result cell sits in column **N** (each N-cell carries a named range `Sch115TD.*`). There is no lettered "SI.No" numbering on the sheet for these lines except the `4(i)/4(ii)/4(iii)/4(iv)` sub-items in column E (`[E9]..[E12]`, mirrored in `[M9]..[M12]`); the CBDT rules number them 1..12 (see rules 757–763), which is the numbering used below.
- **A challan table** headed at `[F21]` *"Date(s) of deposit of tax on accreted income"* with a column header at `r22` and repeating input rows **r23:r29** (7 pre-built lines, extendable) — the array `DepositofTaxAccInc.DepositofTaxAccIncDtls[]`.
- Columns **Q:Z** on rows 16–30 are a **hidden interest-computation engine** (115TE month-diff, 1%/month roll-forward) — helper columns, not schema fields, not built as inputs.
- Feeds **Part B-TTI Sr.no.12** "Net tax payable on 115TD income including interest u/s 115TE" (rule 762) — must equal field 12 of this schedule.

## The rules the sheet computes (with cell references)

- **Net value of assets (field 3)** `[N8]=MAX(0, Sch115TD.AggrFMV − Sch115TD.TotalLiability)` — field 3 = 1 − 2. (rule 757)
- **Total 4(iv)** `[N12]=MAX(0, (Sch115TD.FMV_10_1 + Sch115TD.FMV_12AA + Sch115TD.FMV_115TD_2))` — 4(iv) = 4i + 4ii + 4iii. (rule 758)
- **Accreted income (field 6)** `[N14]=MAX(0, (Sch115TD.NetValueAssets − (Sch115TD.Total − Sch115TD.AssetsLiability)))` — field 6 = [3 − (4(iv) − 5)]. (rule 759)
- **Additional income-tax at MMR (field 7)** `[N15]=ROUND(MAX(0, (Sch115TD.AccretedIncome × 34.944)/100), 0)` — tax at the maximum marginal rate **34.944%**, rounded to rupee.
- **Interest u/s 115TE (field 8)** `[N16]=IFERROR(MAX(0, IntTE), 0)`; `[X16]=SUM(Sch115TD.Amount)`; `[V16]=sheet9.Date` — interest fed from the Q:Z engine (1% per month, see below).
- **Additional income-tax and interest (field 10)** `[N18]=SUM(Sch115TD.AdditionalIncome + Sch115TD.Interest115TE)` — field 10 = 7 + 8.
- **Tax and interest paid (field 11)** `[N19]=MAX(0, SUM(Sch115TD.Amount))` — sum of challan table col K (`[X19]` rounddown note "added by Riyaz SIT-121979").
- **Net payable/refundable (field 12)** `[N20]=MAX(0, ROUNDUP(Sch115TD.AddnIncomeTaxPayable − Sch115TD.TaxPaid, −1))` — field 12 = 10 − 11, rounded **up to nearest 10**. (rule 760)
- **Liability-5 gating** `[V13]=IF(Sch115TD.AccretedIncome<=0, 0, 1)` — field 5 input active only when accreted income > 0.
- **Presence flags** `[V6]=IF(N6="",0,1)`, `[V7]=IF(N7="",0,1)`.

### Interest u/s 115TE engine (hidden helper cols, 1% per month)

- **Specified-date parts** `[Q18]=VALUE(MID(Sch115TD_Date,7,4))` (year), `[R18]=VALUE(MID(Sch115TD_Date,1,2))` (day), `[S18]=VALUE(MID(Sch115TD_Date,4,2))` (month).
- **Month diff** `[T18]=monthdiffTE(Sch115TD_Date, G23)` (custom VBA `monthdiffTE`).
- **New specified date (due date)** `[U18]=IF(Sch115TD_Date="", DATE(2022,4,1), DATE(Q18,S18,R18)+14)` — specified date **+ 14 days**; `[V18]=DformatTE(AC18,"DD/MM/YYYY")`.
- **Rounddown base** `[X18]=ROUNDDOWN(Sch115TD.AdditionalIncome, −2)`.
- **Per-challan roll-forward (r23:r30)**: `[Q23]=MAX(0, IF(OR(K23="",K23=0), monthdiffTE(DueDateTE, sheet9.Date), monthdiffTE(DueDateTE, X23)))` (months of default); `[R23]=ROUND((OBTE−0) × 0.01 × Q23, 0)` — **interest = outstanding × 1% × months**; `[S23]=IF(OR(K23="",K23=0), 0, MAX(0, Z23 − MIN(Z23, R23)))` (tax paid after adjusting interest); `[T23]=MAX(0, OBTE − S23)` (outstanding remaining); `[U23]=MAX(0, R23 − MIN(K23, R23))` (interest carried forward); `[V23]=T23+U23` (outstanding at end); subsequent rows r24:r29 chain off the prior row's `V`/`X`; `[R30]=ROUND(INDIRECT("V"&(LastRows_Table)) × 0.01 × Q30, 0)` closes the table using `LastRows_Table`.

### Validation rules (from rules.json & vba_text)

- **n.757** (cat A): field '3' Net value of assets = Sl.no.1 − Sl.no.2.
- **n.758** (cat A): field '4(iv)' Total = 4i + 4ii + 4iii.
- **n.759** (cat A): field '6' Accreted income = [3 − (4(iv) − 5)].
- **n.760** (cat A): field '12' Net payable/refundable = [10 − 11].
- **n.761** (cat A): if Accreted income u/s 115TD entered and Sr.no.9 "Specified date u/s 115TD" is blank → error (specified date mandatory).
- **n.762** (cat A): Part B-TTI Sr.no.12 "Net tax payable on 115TD income including interest u/s 115TE" should match Sr.no.12 of Schedule 115TD.
- **n.763** (cat A): income entered in return and tax not computed on the same → error.
- **Specified date window** (vba): "Specified date u/s 115TD" must be a valid dd/mm/yyyy within the financial year (FY 2025-26: `2025-04-01`..`2026-03-31`); an out-of-year date is rejected.
- **Challan row validations** (vba `Validatesheet115TD`): Date compulsory & not after system date; Bank name required, special chars `< > & ' "` disallowed; BSR code required, "First 3 numeric and next 4 alphanumeric"; Serial number of challan required, digits only; Amount deposited required, digits only. "Total of Point no 11 should not be more than 14 digits."

## What this means for the build

- Build **two enterable numbers** (fields 1 and 2) and the three **4(i)/4(ii)/4(iii)** FMV inputs, plus field 5 (liability on assets at 4). Everything else in the column is computed:
  - field 3 = MAX(0, 1 − 2); field 4(iv) = MAX(0, 4i+4ii+4iii); field 6 (accreted income) = MAX(0, 3 − (4(iv) − 5)).
  - field 7 = ROUND(accreted income × **34.944%**, 0) — additional income-tax at MMR.
  - field 8 = interest u/s 115TE via the 1%/month engine keyed off the **specified date + 14 days** due date and each challan's deposit date.
  - field 10 = 7 + 8; field 11 = Σ challan amounts; field 12 = MAX(0, ROUNDUP(10 − 11, −1)).
- Require the **Specified date** (field 9) whenever accreted income > 0, validate dd/mm/yyyy within FY 2025-26, and store as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Build the challan table as a repeating group mapping to `DepositofTaxAccIncDtls[]`; auto-number SI.No, validate BSR (3 numeric + 4 alphanumeric), challan serial (digits, ≤5), amount (digits), bank name (≤125, disallow `< > & ' "`), and deposit date (not future).
- Push field 12 to **Part B-TTI Sr.no.12** (rule 762): "Net tax payable on 115TD income including interest u/s 115TE" must equal it.
