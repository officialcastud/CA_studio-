auto-computed by `engOs()` into green (untypeable) cells — every "X = sum of …" total (1,

per-nature caps (514–520, 544), (b) the item-10 quarterly reconciliations except winnings
(526, 534–538, 540, 545, 546, 547), and (c) a few cross-schedule / option-flag checks (528,
543 refinement, 550).
