# ITR-3 · Category A rules 701–750 — enforcement audit
read-only · opus auditor

Scope: Schedule 80D (health insurance), 80E/80EE/80EEA/80EEB (loans), 80-IA/IB/IE (VI-A cross-checks), and the 80C+80CCC+80CCD(1) cap. The six unencoded 80D rules (713, 715, 716, 720, 723, 726) are all enforced by construction in the exporter/engine `forms/ITR-3/src/70_sec_ded.js`, not by an `A()` call — see findings.

Key structural mechanism (80D): the UI (70_sec_ded.js:322–331) offers a single senior-citizen dropdown per side with options No / Yes / "Not claiming"; `engDed80D()` (70_sec_ded.js:118–135) routes a single entered total into 1a-vs-1b (self) or 2a-vs-2b (parents) by that flag and zeroes the side when "Not claiming"; the exporter (70_sec_ded.js:467–479) writes the amounts, the premium = sum of insurance rows (`aHI/bHI/cHI/dHI = ins(...)`, 70_sec_ded.js:120), and always emits InsurerName/PolicyNo with a "NA" fallback (70_sec_ded.js:466).
