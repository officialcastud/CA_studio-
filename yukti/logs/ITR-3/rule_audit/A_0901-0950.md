read-only · opus auditor

Note on the Part B-TI family (914–949): every Part B-TI cell is written by `expTax()` in
`70_sec_tax.js` (`put(j,"PartB-TI.…",n0(T.…))`, lines 451–488) from the engine's own computed
values, and rendered as green untypeable cells — the user cannot type them. Cross-links whose
Part B-TI side is one of those computed cells are therefore enforced by construction (STRUCT)
even when no `A()` call exists; where the team *also* added an `A()` internal-consistency check
it is marked OK.
