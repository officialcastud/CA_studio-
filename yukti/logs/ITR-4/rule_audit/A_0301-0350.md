# ITR-4 · Category A rules 301–350 — enforcement audit
read-only · opus auditor

Rule engine batches live in `forms/ITR-4/src/61_rules_g0.js`, `61_rules_g1.js`, `61_rules_g2.js`
(`60_rules.js` is the runRules wrapper, no A() calls). Structural enforcement checked in
`70_sec_ded.js` (Chapter VI-A / 80D / HRA), `70_sec_inccore.js` (salary, HP, filing status),
`shell/shell.js` (dte). Key structural fact: the Chapter VI-A **"Allowed"** column is
`out[k]`, derived by starting from the claimed amount and only ever *reducing* it by statutory
caps (`70_sec_ded.js:198-217`), then rendered as a computed green cell (`70_sec_ded.js:326`);
so `Allowed ≤ You-claim` holds by construction for every VI-A line — this is what makes the
"eligible ≤ user-enterable" family (324-342) structural even where no A() call exists.
