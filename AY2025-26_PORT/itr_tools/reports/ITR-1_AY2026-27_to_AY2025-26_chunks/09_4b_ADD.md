### 4b. ADD — rules that exist only in 2025-26

- **A-19** (p7): ITR-1 -"Name" of taxpayer in ITR does not match with the "Name" as per the PAN data base
- **A-42** (p8): Sec 10(26AAA)-Any income as referred to in section 10(26AAA) drop-down cannot be selected more than one time under Exempt Income.
- **A-59** (p8): Gross salary should be total of salary as per section 17(1) and value of perquisites as per section 17(2) and profits in lieu of salary as per section 17(3) and Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country u/s 89A and Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a country other than notified country u/s 89A
- **A-88** (p10): If Old Tax Regime is selected, then Deduction u/s 80G is not allowed for donation made in cash above Rs. 2,000/-.
- **A-118** (p11): In schedule 80G, if donation is made same PAN of Donee cannot appear more than once
- **A-186** (p15): In B(i)- Salary, Income claimed for relief from taxation u/s 89A cannot be claimed more than income offered in B(i)(d)
- **A-187** (p15): In B3- Income from other sources, Income claimed for relief from taxation u/s 89A cannot be claimed more than the income offered under Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country u/s 89A
- **A-188** (p15): Sum of quarterly breakup of Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country u/s 89A (taxable portion) should be equal to 'amount entered in Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country u/s 89A- Income claimed for relief from taxation u/s 89A'
- **A-189** (p15): Interest from Income Tax Refund drop-down cannot be selected more than one time under Income from other sources.
- **A-190** (p15): Interest from Income Tax Refund drop-down cannot be selected more than one time under Income from other sources.
- **A-191** (p15): In Schedule Income from Other sources, under dropdown, "Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country u/s 89A " in country drop-down, one country cannot be selected more than one time.
- **A-192** (p15): In Schedule Salary, under line item "Income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country u/s 89A " in country drop-down, one country cannot be selected more than one time.
- **A-219** (p16): When details of Salary Income are given then 'Nature of employment' cannot be selected as 'Not Applicable',
- **A-235** (p17): "Details of Bank from which loan is taken" need to be provided for claiming deduction u/s 80E in schedule 80E
- **A-248** (p18): Form 10IA filed for autism, cerebral palsy, multiple disabilities needs to be filed separately for claiming Deduction u/s 80U and 80DD respectively.
- **A-272** (p19): HRA u/s 10(13A) shall not be more than 50% of basic salary and DA
- **D-1** (p20): Under Salary, assessee has claimed Income claimed for relief from taxation u/s 89A without furnishing of Form 10EE.
- **D-2** (p20): Under Income from Other Sources, assessee has claimed Income claimed for relief from taxation u/s 89A without furnishing of Form 10EE.
- **D-4** (p20): Deduction u/s 80GG shall be claimed for the period of stay for which the rent is actually paid, i.e., 5000 per month
